Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to develop software?
Software development costs depend on feature complexity and platform requirements. We provide transparent quotations based on detailed requirements breakdown. Simple showcase projects start from a few thousand pounds, whilst complex systems are assessed based on working hours.
What is the typical development timeline?
Simple applications or corporate websites typically take 2-4 weeks. Medium to large bespoke systems usually require 2-3 months. We create strict timelines to ensure on-time delivery.
Do you provide after-sales maintenance?
Yes, we offer a standard one-year free technical maintenance period, including bug fixes and basic server maintenance. Extended maintenance services are available after the initial period.
